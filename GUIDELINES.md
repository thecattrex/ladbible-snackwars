## Editing spec
- Vertical 9:16, faces centred, no zoomed-out frame
- Hook on screen in the first 2 s, guest name on screen in the first second
- Big burned-in captions, spelled right, swears bleeped like the episode
- Hard cuts and punch-ins on reactions, no flashy transitions
- Music allowed low under the talking; no phonk, no meme edits, no filters or colour changes
- Mix: 15–30 s punch clips, 30–60 s story clips, full rounds up to 3 min

## Never
- Edit what they said, or put a reaction on a different snack
- Make John or Jessica look bad unless they are clearly in on the joke
- Hooks the clip does not deliver on
- Mention movies, WWE or personal lives unless they said it in this episode
- Repost LADbible's clips or another clipper's edit
- AI: auto clippers, AI voices, AI b-roll
- Swearing, politics or sexual content in your text
- Buy views/likes, hide likes, or submit the same link to two campaigns
